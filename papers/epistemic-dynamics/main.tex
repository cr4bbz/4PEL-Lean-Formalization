\begin{abstract}
This paper develops a formally verified account of fallible epistemic agency within 4PEL, a four-valued framework that distinguishes strict support, strict rejection, contradiction, and evidential gap. The formal development begins with recovery and path-dependent update, extends to active sensing and decision-theoretic information acquisition, and then lifts uncertainty from the world to the observation model itself. The Gate-95--104 sequence formalizes one-step policy self-correction, model-class misspecification, predictive surprise, explicit outside-class probability mass, meta-level gap and contradiction, and a nested world--model self-trust controller. Gates 105--109 turn the nested state into a repair dynamics: all sixteen canonical world--model statuses are reachable under an explicit coordinate-update relation, epistemic maintenance becomes cost-sensitive, qualitative four-valued compression is shown not to be universally control-sufficient, concrete repair witnesses admit a strictly decreasing local potential, and a finite repair controller satisfies a conditional liveness theorem under a successful-repair oracle. Gates 110--115 expose the informational premise behind that oracle. Observational aliasing makes uniformly correct repair impossible when target-different worlds share an observation; an active separating experiment can restore a truth guarantee; identifiability itself has a cost; preserving the four-valued gap $N$ can be value-optimal; and a general theorem proves that a truth-guaranteeing decoder exists exactly when the requested target is constant on every observation fiber. A minimal experiment witness then shows that target identification can succeed without full hidden-world identification. Gate 116 removes deterministic observation and introduces a known noisy sensor. Bayesian posterior confidence can cross an explicit four-valued control threshold after repeated agreeing observations even though the alternative world retains positive likelihood. The resulting architecture separates hidden truth, quantitative belief, qualitative four-valued diagnosis, and control commitment. All claims described as verified are mechanized in Lean 4; finite modelling choices and philosophical interpretations are stated separately from theorem-level results.
\end{abstract}
