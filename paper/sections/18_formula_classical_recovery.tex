\section{Formula-level classical recovery and operator boundaries}
\label{sec:formula-classical-recovery}

Gate~5 isolated the classical value sector by structural conditions on coarse
evidence.  A separate question is whether that sector is compositional at the
object-language level.  This section answers the propositional part positively,
identifies a sharp boundary at Lockean belief, and proves preservation for the
primitive knowledge and possibility transports on classical accessible profiles.

The distinction is important.  From
\[
  v\in\{\Tval,\Fval\}
\]
it does not follow merely by terminology that every compound expression evaluated
from \(v\) is classical.  The preservation property must be checked constructor by
constructor.

\subsection{Structural induction on the propositional fragment}

Let \texttt{Formula.IsPropositional} denote the fragment generated by atoms,
negation, and conjunction, excluding the Lockean belief constructor.  The existing
OR and material-implication forms are syntactic macros built from those constructors
and therefore remain inside the fragment.

At a world \(w\), write \(\mathsf{AtomicClassicalAt}(m,w)\) when every atomic
valuation belongs to \(\{\Tval,\Fval\}\).

\begin{theorem}[Propositional formula recovery]
\label{thm:formula-classical-recovery}
If every atom is classical at \(w\) and \(\varphi\) is propositional, then
\[
  \llbracket\varphi\rrbracket_{m,w}\in\{\Tval,\Fval\}.
\]
\statusverified
\end{theorem}

The proof is structural induction.  The atomic case is the hypothesis; the negation
and conjunction steps use the Gate~5 closure theorems for the recovered classical
slice.  No second truth-value algebra is introduced.

Two familiar classical laws therefore lift from values to formulas on this restricted
sector.

\begin{corollary}[Formula-level excluded middle]
\label{cor:formula-lem-recovery}
Under the hypotheses of Theorem~\ref{thm:formula-classical-recovery},
\[
  \llbracket\varphi\lor\neg\varphi\rrbracket_{m,w}=\Tval.
\]
\statusverified
\end{corollary}

\begin{corollary}[Restricted formula-level explosion]
\label{cor:formula-efq-recovery}
Under the same hypotheses,
\[
  \llbracket\varphi\land\neg\varphi\rrbracket_{m,w}=\Fval,
\]
so the contradiction value LP-validly implies any conclusion value at that
model/world.  This is a recovery theorem relative to regular propositional
valuations, not unrestricted LP entailment in arbitrary 4-PEL models.
\statusverified
\end{corollary}

\subsection{Lockean belief can reopen a gap}

Atomic bivalence is insufficient once the probabilistic belief constructor is
admitted.  Gate~6 contains a two-world witness whose only atomic values are
\(\Tval\) and \(\Fval\).  The accessible profile splits the positive and negative
extensions evenly, with mass \(1/2\) on each side and threshold \(3/5\).  Therefore
neither threshold is reached:
\[
  \Bel p = \Nval.
\]

\begin{proposition}[Classical atoms do not force classical belief]
\label{prop:belief-reopens-gap}
There exists a finite 4-PEL model whose atomic valuation is classical at every world
but in which a belief formula evaluates to \(\Nval\).
\statuswitness
\end{proposition}

The witness separates two kinds of nonclassicality.  No contradictory support is
needed.  The belief gap is generated by probabilistic indecision:
\[
  P^+(p)<c
  \qquad\text{and}\qquad
  P^-(p)<c.
\]
In the concrete witness both inequalities are \(1/2<3/5\).

\subsection{The exact local belief-recovery condition}

To expose this boundary, Gate~6 names the positive and negative threshold decisions
made by the existing belief implementation.  Write \(b_+\) and \(b_-\) for these
Boolean decisions.  Define
\begin{align*}
  \mathsf{BelComplete} &:\Longleftrightarrow b_+\lor b_-,\\
  \mathsf{BelConsistent} &:\Longleftrightarrow \neg(b_+\land b_-),\\
  \mathsf{BelRegular} &:\Longleftrightarrow
    \mathsf{BelComplete}\land\mathsf{BelConsistent}.
\end{align*}

\begin{theorem}[Exact Lockean recovery boundary]
\label{thm:belief-threshold-regular}
For every model, agent, world, and FDE-valued input profile,
\[
  \Bel(v)\in\{\Tval,\Fval\}
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  \mathsf{BelRegular}(v).
\]
Equivalently, exactly one of the two threshold decisions must fire.
\statusverified
\end{theorem}

The balanced Gate~6 witness is formally classified as threshold-incomplete but
threshold-consistent.  Thus its failure of classicality is specifically a failure of
coverage at the belief threshold, not a glut.

Theorem~\ref{thm:belief-threshold-regular} is exact but deliberately local.  It does
not yet derive threshold consistency from finite additivity plus \(c>1/2\).  Such a
derivation requires the stronger \texttt{FiniteProbabilityIntegrity} layer together
with a proof that the positive and negative events form the relevant complementary
partition.  The lightweight legacy \texttt{Model} interface is too weak for that
step, and no stronger probabilistic conclusion is inferred from it here.

\subsection{Knowledge and possibility preserve classical profiles}

The conservative modal language contains primitive evidence-stable knowledge and raw
accessibility possibility.  Unlike Lockean belief, both preserve the recovered
classical sector when their accessible semantic input profile is already classical.

\begin{theorem}[Modal transport preservation]
\label{thm:modal-classical-preservation}
Let \(v:W\to\{\Nval,\Tval,\Fval,\Bval\}\).  If every world accessible from
\(w\) has \(v(u)\in\{\Tval,\Fval\}\), then both
\[
  \mathsf{Poss}_i(v)(w)
  \quad\text{and}\quad
  \Know_i(v)(w)
\]
belong to \(\{\Tval,\Fval\}\).
\statusverified
\end{theorem}

For raw possibility, classicality turns the negative bit into the Boolean complement
of the positive bit across the finite accessibility list.  For evidence-stable
knowledge, an unstable profile collapses to \(\Fval\), while a stable classical
profile retains a classical result.  The theorem also covers the repository's empty
accessibility conventions.

This yields a stronger compositional statement for the modal language.

\begin{theorem}[Belief-free modal recovery]
\label{thm:belief-free-modal-recovery}
Suppose every atomic value at every world is classical.  Then every modal formula
built from atoms, negation, conjunction, primitive knowledge, and primitive raw
possibility, but containing no Lockean belief constructor, evaluates in
\(\{\Tval,\Fval\}\) at every world.
\statusverified
\end{theorem}

Hence the first operator boundary of formula-level classical recovery is not
``modality'' in general.  It is specifically the thresholding operation used by
Lockean belief.

\subsection{A recursive recovery contract for the full legacy syntax}

Gate~6 packages the preceding result into a semantic admissibility predicate
\texttt{Formula.ClassicalRecoveryAdmissibleAt}.  Atoms must be classical; negation
and conjunction recurse without new obligations.  A belief node requires two things:
its subformula must remain recovery-admissible throughout the accessible range, and
the local belief threshold pair must be regular.

\begin{theorem}[Full legacy-formula recovery under admissibility]
\label{thm:full-formula-recovery}
For every legacy formula \(\varphi\),
\[
  \mathsf{ClassicalRecoveryAdmissibleAt}(m,w,\varphi)
  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  \llbracket\varphi\rrbracket_{m,w}\in\{\Tval,\Fval\}.
\]
\statusverified
\end{theorem}

This is the first formula-level version of the classical-recovery hypothesis.  It
should not be read as an unrestricted theorem that all 4-PEL formulas are classical.
Instead it identifies the constructor-local obligations required for classicality to
survive composition.

\subsection{Relation to the existing CPEL translation}

The repository already defines positive and negative translations
\texttt{tr\_pos} and \texttt{tr\_neg} into a classical probabilistic epistemic
syntax.  The accompanying source comment sketches a completeness reduction, but no
Lean theorem presently proves that reduction.  Gate~6 therefore does not use that
comment as evidence for completeness.

A subsequent step can compare the direct recovery theorem above with the split
translation and ask whether regular valuations force a collapse or correspondence
between the positive and negative classical translations.  Until such a theorem is
proved, the mechanized result is the direct semantic recovery developed here.

\paragraph{Gate boundary.}
Gate~6 establishes compositional recovery and isolates the Lockean threshold as a
special source of nonclassicality.  Two stronger questions remain open: deriving
belief regularity from probability-integrity hypotheses, and proving a genuine
proof-system or CPEL-translation completeness theorem for the recovered fragment.
