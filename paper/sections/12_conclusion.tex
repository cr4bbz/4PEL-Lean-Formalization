\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The dynamic 4-PEL development begins with a simple threshold semantics and ends with a surprisingly structured phase picture. Positive and negative evidence are tracked independently, so probabilistic belief is a pair of Lockean threshold decisions. Evidence-stable knowledge is exactly that belief value filtered by qualitative homogeneity of the complete accessible FDE profile.

Admissible conditionalization changes only probability, yet its categorical effects are maximally expressive. A six-world family realizes every ordered transition between \(\Tval,\Fval,\Bval,\Nval\) at the level of \(\Know(\Bel p)\). Gluts and gaps can both be created and removed. This global reachability does not make the dynamics arbitrary. Pointwise belief invariance is characterized exactly by preservation of the two threshold bits.

Those bits supply a natural Boolean geometry. The four FDE values form a threshold square, and the number of changed coordinates gives a wall count of zero, one, or two. Numerical endpoint analysis then identifies a changed bit with straddling of the corresponding support threshold. The affine interpolation of rational support masses turns straddling into a literal threshold-hit theorem with an explicit parameter in the unit interval.

For diagonal two-wall transitions, the two support coordinates have unique crossing times. Their order adds temporal structure absent from the endpoints. Simultaneous crossing is exactly passage through \((c,c)\). Sequential crossing forces an adjacent intermediate phase at the rational midpoint. Consequently, a diagonal endpoint transition has a short hidden phase history. For \(\Nval\to\Bval\), positive-first motion yields \(\Nval\to\Tval\to\Bval\), while negative-first motion yields \(\Nval\to\Fval\to\Bval\). The remaining diagonals obey the corresponding symmetric table.

The probability layer can now also be given a stronger constructive foundation. Nonnegative normalized rational world weights generate finite measures satisfying explicit integrity laws. The rational segment between two such distributions remains inside the finite probability simplex, and the mass of every fixed event moves affinely. These interpolated local distributions can be packaged into complete \texttt{StrongProbabilityModel} paths while worlds, accessibility, valuation, and threshold remain fixed.

The main verified dependency chain is therefore
\[
\boxed{
\text{finite probability integrity}
\to
\text{probability simplex}
\to
\text{strong model path}
\to
\text{affine fixed-event mass}
\to
\text{threshold side}
\to
\text{categorical wall}
\to
\text{crossing order}
\to
\text{intermediate FDE phase}.}
\]

This chain turns the four-valued endpoint space into more than a classification table. It gives the beginning of a kinematics of epistemic phases in which a genuine model-valued probability path can project to a piecewise categorical FDE history.

The remaining lifting problem is now sharper than before. Model existence along the rational convex path is verified for weight-generated strong endpoints, but the positive and negative support events of an arbitrary probability-sensitive modal formula need not remain fixed as the model varies. The next step is therefore to characterize a path-invariant formula fragment, beginning with atomic and probability-free formulas, and to show that its signed belief-support masses inherit the verified affine event-mass theorem. A separate question asks when the intermediate models can themselves be generated by admissible conditionalization rather than merely connected by a valid convex probability path.

Only after those formula-level and update-level distinctions are settled should the development move to arbitrary continuous paths or general measure spaces. The finite rational theory already establishes complete reachability, exact robustness, wall geometry, event order, forced intermediate phases, finite probability integrity, and strong model-valued convex paths, while keeping stronger topological and literature claims explicit rather than hidden inside the formal results.
