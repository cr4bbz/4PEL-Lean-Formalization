# Finite Fine-Grainedness and 4-PEL

## Research question

Can the classical impossibility of a satisfactory population axiology be
reinterpreted as the existence of non-trivial four-valued comparison states,
rather than as the non-existence of every adequate comparison system?

This branch begins with structural results only. It does **not** yet claim a
formalization of Arrhenius's six theorems or Thornley's risky variants.

## Two simplexes, two roles

The probability simplex and the 4-PEL evidence simplex must not be identified.

| Layer | Point represents | Vertices / coordinates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outcome simplex | a population prospect | possible populations |
| Evidence simplex | evidential status of a comparison | `T`, `B`, `N`, `F` |

A later kernel may connect them by mapping each population outcome, or each
reason generated by that outcome, to a four-cell evidence profile. The present
gate proves the structural facts needed before that semantic choice is fixed.

## Hypotheses

### H1 - FFG is finite-path connectivity - verified at the definition layer

For a slight-difference relation `S` on welfare levels, Finite
Fine-Grainedness is represented as

```text
forall x y, FiniteStepChain S x y.
```

This exposes FFG as the claim that the directed small-step graph has one finite
reachability component.

### H2 - Transitivity globalizes local compensation - Lean-verified

If a comparison relation is reflexive and transitive and validates every
slight step, then it validates every comparison connected by a finite slight
chain. Under FFG, the transport becomes global.

This theorem isolates the Sorites-shaped engine used by finite-chain
impossibility arguments without yet importing substantive population axioms.

### H3 - Lexicality blocks the transport bridge - Lean-verified countermodel

A two-tier welfare carrier is defined in which slight steps preserve tier.
Every finite chain preserves tier, so no chain connects the higher and lower
tiers. Hence FFG fails.

The result is structural: it does not yet assert that any specific good is
lexically higher.

### H4 - Classical contradiction can become an axiological glut - Lean-verified

Positive support for `x >= y` and negative support for that same comparison are
stored independently. If finite-chain transport yields the positive side while
another condition yields rejection, `comparisonValue` is exactly `B`.

An explicit witness also verifies the non-explosion boundary: the target
comparison is `B` while an unrelated claim remains `N`, not `T`.

This does not refute a classical impossibility theorem. It replaces its target
object - a consistent total ordering - with a non-trivial four-valued evidence
semantics for comparisons.

### H5 - Probability paths do not force bivalence - Lean-verified witness

Two affine positive/negative support paths have the same thresholded endpoints

```text
T -> F
```

but different midpoint values:

```text
exclusive support path   -> N
overlapping support path -> B.
```

Therefore classical endpoint data does not determine the internal phase. A
probability-simplex path can geometrically connect endpoints while retaining
either underdetermination or contradiction.

### H6 - Risk repairs connectivity but not semantic consistency - open

Thornley's move to population prospects may restore finite interpolation by
varying probabilities. The 4-PEL hypothesis is that this restores a path in the
prospect space without guaranteeing that the induced comparison is classical.

The exact theorem requires:

1. a faithful Lean encoding of the risky adequacy conditions;
2. a typed separation between population outcomes, prospects, and comparison
   evidence;
3. a theorem identifying which inconsistent classical conclusions become `B`;
4. a decision/extraction policy showing what practical recommendations, if
   any, are licensed by `B` and `N`.

### H7 - The contradiction may live in a forgotten fibre - open

The midpoint witness suggests a fibre phenomenon already familiar from the
4-PEL simplex program: identical coarse endpoint values can have different
evidential lifts. A future theorem should characterize the fibres of the map
from support paths to thresholded endpoint pairs and determine when those
fibres contain both gap-midpoint and glut-midpoint realizations.

## Formalization boundary

The verified core currently establishes:

- finite-chain connectivity and transport;
- a lexical disconnected countermodel;
- paraconsistent recoding of simultaneous support and rejection;
- a concrete gap/glut path-lift separation.

It does not yet establish:

- that all of Arrhenius's adequacy conditions are jointly satisfiable in a
  4-PEL model;
- that Thornley's exact risky theorem yields `B` under a faithful translation;
- that a four-valued ordering is sufficient for rational choice;
- novelty relative to existing paraconsistent social-choice or population-
  ethics literature.

## Next gate

Formalize a minimal population-prospect signature and one exact risky
compensation condition. The first target should be a small finite witness, not
the full Sixth Impossibility Theorem. The gate succeeds only if Lean verifies
both the classical conflict projection and its non-trivial four-valued lift.
