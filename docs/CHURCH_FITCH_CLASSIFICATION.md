## Axes

4-PEL separates two choices that classical presentations often identify.

### Truth axis

1. **positive/designated truth**

   `phi.pos = true`

2. **strict truth**

   `phi = T`

Strict truth implies positive truth, but not conversely.

### Possibility axis

1. **primitive raw possibility**

   `Diamond_raw psi`

   Positive raw possibility is witness-bearing: it exposes an accessible world
   at which `psi.pos = true`.

2. **internal knowledge dual**

   `not K(not psi)`

   This need not be witness-bearing. Under accessible FDE instability it can
   become strict `T` even when raw positive possibility is absent.

## Verified possibility asymmetry

Primitive raw possibility implies positive internal-dual possibility:

```text
Diamond_raw+(psi) -> (not K(not psi))+
```

The converse fails. In the finite `FitchDualModel`:

```text

K p(gap) = N
K p(falseWorld) = F
```

so at the root:

```text

not K(not K p)  = T.
```

Thus dualized "knowability" can be generated by epistemic instability without
any accessible positive knowledge witness.
